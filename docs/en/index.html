About LiquiCool Technology

Professional Liquid Cooling Technology Team

LiquiCool Technology was founded by Professor Wang Zuankai's team from Hong Kong Polytechnic University, specializing in R&D and application of data center liquid cooling solutions.

We are committed to providing customers with energy-efficient thermal management technology, achieving intelligent temperature control management for data centers through innovative structural thermal armor technology.

Our solutions feature high energy efficiency (PUE < 1.06), high water efficiency (WUE < 0.5), and low carbon emissions.
